Who We Are

Building India's Intelligent Emergency Response Ecosystem

SkyFly Aviations is an Indian HealthTech startup focused on emergency response innovation. Our first product AeroLifeLine helps hospitals, patients, and ambulance providers coordinate emergencies faster using AI and cloud technology.

Instead of waiting years to launch autonomous aircraft, we decided to solve today's emergency coordination challenges first. This approach creates immediate impact while building the digital infrastructure for tomorrow's future air medical mobility.

The Challenge

Every Minute Matters

India's emergency response system faces critical challenges that cost lives every day.

Traffic Congestion

Ambulances get stuck in urban traffic, losing precious minutes during golden hour emergencies.

Delayed Ambulances

Average ambulance response time exceeds critical windows due to poor dispatch coordination.

Poor Coordination

No unified platform connecting patients, hospitals, and ambulance providers in real time.

Manual Phone Calls

Emergency requests still rely on fragmented phone calls, causing delays and miscommunication.

Location Confusion

Inaccurate location sharing leads to ambulances arriving at wrong addresses.

Incomplete Information

Hospitals receive incomplete patient data, unable to prepare adequately before arrival.

Every second counts in an emergency. We're fixing this.

How It Works

From Emergency to Arrival

AeroLifeLine orchestrates the entire emergency response workflow in seconds.

Patient Requests Emergency

One tap SOS from the patient mobile app triggers an urgent alert with location and medical info.

GPS Captured

Precise geographic coordinates are captured and transmitted to the cloud platform instantly.

Hospital Notified

Nearest hospitals receive real-time alert with patient details, location, and estimated severity.

Nearest Ambulance Assigned

AI dispatch algorithm selects the closest available ambulance based on real-time traffic conditions.

Driver Navigation

Ambulance driver receives optimized route with live traffic updates and hospital destination.

Hospital Prepares

Emergency room receives ETA, patient vitals, and prepares medical team for immediate care.
